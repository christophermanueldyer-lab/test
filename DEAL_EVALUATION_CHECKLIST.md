## 💰 REVENUE MODEL

**Type:**
☐ Recurring subscriptions (IDEAL)
☐ Service contracts (GOOD)

☐ One-time sales (CAUTION)
☐ Project-based (CAUTION)

**Customer Retention:**
- Annual retention rate: _____%
- ☐ >85% (IDEAL) ☐ 70-85% (OK) ☐ <70% (CAUTION)

**Customer Concentration:**
- Top customer: ____% of revenue
- Top 3 customers: ____% of revenue
- ☐ No customer >15% (IDEAL)
- ☐ Top 3 <30% (OK)
- ☐ High concentration (RED FLAG)

**Revenue Predictability:**
☐ Can forecast next 6 months accurately
☐ Recurring revenue >50% of total
☐ Contracts/agreements in place

## 👔 MANAGEMENT (CRITICAL FOR SEMI-ABSENTEE)

**Manager Status:**
☐ Manager in place running day-to-day (REQUIRED)
☐ Manager tenure >2 years (IDEAL)
☐ Manager willing to stay post-sale (REQUIRED)
☐ Manager not owner's family (PREFERRED)

**Owner Time Investment:**
- Owner works _____ hours/week currently
- ☐ <15 hours/week (IDEAL for semi-absentee)
- ☐ 15-25 hours/week (OK if transferable)
- ☐ >25 hours/week (CAUTION - may not be semi-absentee)

**Team:**
- Number of employees: _____
- ☐ 5-50 employees (good range)
- ☐ Low turnover
- ☐ Key employees documented

**Manager Red Flags:**
☐ Manager wants to leave
☐ Owner can't take 2-week vacation
☐ High employee turnover
☐ No clear second-in-command

## ⚠️ RED FLAGS

Check if ANY of these are present:

☐ Revenue declining 2 of last 3 years
☐ No manager or manager leaving
☐ Owner deeply embedded in operations
☐ Customer concentration >30% (top 3)
☐ Can't verify financials
☐ Financials don't match tax returns
☐ Pending litigation
☐ Regulatory/compliance issues
☐ Single supplier dependency
☐ Industry in structural decline
☐ Owner won't provide transition support
☐ Excessive seller "add-backs" to inflate SDE
☐ High employee turnover
☐ Negative online reviews (if applicable)
☐ My gut says something is off

**If ANY red flags checked:** ☐ Walk away ☐ Dig deeper (only if minor)

---

## ✅ GREEN FLAGS

Count how many apply:

☐ 3+ years consistent profitability
☐ Growing or stable revenue
☐ Strong manager in place >2 years
☐ Manager excited about new ownership
☐ Recurring revenue >50%
☐ High customer retention (>85%)
☐ Diversified customer base
☐ Documented processes
☐ Owner works <15 hours/week
☐ Seller willing to finance 50%+
☐ Clean financials that verify
☐ Good online reputation
☐ Fits my target industries
☐ I understand the business model
☐ Room for improvement/growth
☐ My gut feels good

**Green Flag Score:** _____ / 16

- 12-16: EXCELLENT - Pursue aggressively
- 8-11: GOOD - Strong interest, investigate further
- 5-7: OK - Worth exploring, but cautiously
- 0-4: PASS - Not the right deal

## 💭 GUT CHECK

**Answer honestly:**

1. Would I be excited to own this business?
   ☐ YES ☐ MAYBE ☐ NO

2. Can I see myself owning this for 5+ years?
   ☐ YES ☐ MAYBE ☐ NO

3. Do the numbers actually work for my situation?
   ☐ YES ☐ MAYBE ☐ NO

4. Is this semi-absentee OR am I compromising?
   ☐ TRULY SEMI-ABSENTEE ☐ COMPROMISING (be honest!)

5. Am I considering this because it's good OR because I'm impatient?
   ☐ IT'S ACTUALLY GOOD ☐ I'M IMPATIENT (be honest!)

If any answer is NO or you're compromising, **think very carefully.**

                          ----------
Pre-tax Cash to Me:       $_________
Taxes (estimate 30%):     $_________
                          ----------
NET ANNUAL CASH TO ME:    $_________

**Is this monthly amount worth it?** ☐ YES ☐ NO

**Remember:** You keep your job income + this passive income + you're building equity

**How to use:**
1. Print 50 copies or fill out digitally

3. File in binder or folder (physical or digital)
4. Compare deals side-by-side
5. Track patterns in what you like/don't like
6. Refine your criteria over time

**After reviewing 20+ businesses:**
- You'll complete this in 15 minutes
- You'll spot red flags instantly
- You'll know your criteria by heart
- You'll be a much smarter buyer
