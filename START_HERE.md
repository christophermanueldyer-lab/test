## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Early Mistakes:**
- ❌ Submitting LOI on first business you see
- ❌ Not reading "Buy Then Build"
- ❌ Skipping attorney/CPA team building
- ❌ Not tracking deals in spreadsheet
- ❌ Analysis paralysis (overthinking everything)

**Mid-Journey Mistakes:**
- ❌ Falling in love with a business
- ❌ Ignoring red flags because you're impatient
- ❌ Not vetting manager thoroughly (critical for semi-absentee!)
- ❌ Accepting "all cash" deal (seller should finance)
- ❌ Skipping due diligence steps

**Late-Stage Mistakes:**
- ❌ Rushing through due diligence
- ❌ Not meeting manager 3+ times
- ❌ Weak purchase agreement (letting attorney phone it in)
- ❌ Inadequate transition period
- ❌ No manager retention agreement
