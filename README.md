## 💡 Key Strategy Points

**Your Ideal Business:**
- Purchase price: $200K-$400K
- Recurring revenue model (subscriptions, contracts, memberships)
- Existing manager in place (2+ years, willing to stay)
- Semi-absentee ready (owner currently works <15 hours/week)
- San Fernando Valley location OR fully online/remote

**Your Ideal Deal Structure:**
- 25% down payment (~$75K from your $100K)
- 75% seller financing (7 years, 6% interest)
- Strong manager retention agreement
- 6-month seller transition support

**Industries That Fit:**
- SaaS/subscription software
- Subscription box services
- Membership-based businesses
- B2B service contracts (recurring)
- Digital marketing agencies (retainer clients)
- Service-based franchises

## ⚠️ Critical Warnings

1. **Don't Rush** - Most buyers review 150-200 businesses before buying one
2. **Manager is Everything** - For semi-absentee, you MUST have a strong manager
3. **Seller Financing is Key** - If seller won't finance, ask why
4. **Trust Your Gut** - The right deal will feel right AND make financial sense
5. **Verify Everything** - In due diligence, trust but verify all claims

This is a **marathon, not a sprint**.

- Better to wait for the right deal than rush into the wrong one

- One bad deal can wipe out years of wealth
- The right deal will eventually appear
